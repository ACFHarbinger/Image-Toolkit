### Agy — 2026-08-23 (Roadmap Critique & Stitch Robustness Proposals)

Critique of `submodules/ASP/docs/moon/asp_change_roadmap_2026q3.md` and ideas
to improve Raw ASP stitch success rate (moving beyond just failure gating):

1. **Background-Masked Matcher Filtering (Root Cause of Alignment Outliers)**:
   - *Problem*: Moving foreground characters contaminate Stage 5/6 matchers (LoFTR/SuperPoint), generating false correspondences across animated poses that pull BA into degenerate affines or disconnected graphs.
   - *Proposal*: Stage 4 already extracts BiRefNet foreground masks. Feed the inverted background mask (`~fg_mask`) into Stage 5 to restrict feature detection/matching to static scene elements. This directly prevents moving cels from corrupting camera trajectory estimation.

2. **OpenCV/Hugin Technique Adaptation (MAGSAC++ & Multi-Band Blending)**:
   - *Estimator upgrade*: Replace standard OpenCV RANSAC with `cv::USAC_MAGSAC` (`cv.findHomography` / `cv.estimateAffine2D` with `cv.USAC_MAGSAC`). MAGSAC++ provides marginalization over noise scales and handles high-outlier ratios without brittle pixel threshold tuning.
   - *Multi-Band / Laplacian Pyramid Blending*: Standard seam cutting in Stage 7 produces visible steps when background plates have subtle lighting/color drift across frames. Adopting multi-band blending (as used in Hugin/Enblend) in non-character background regions smooths low-frequency illumination gradients while preserving high-frequency line art.

3. **Motion Constraint Priors (Rigid 1D/2D Translation Prior)**:
   - Cel animation camera pans are physically executed via linear animation stands (pure vertical $\Delta y$ or horizontal $\Delta x$ with zero in-plane rotation or shear). Enforcing a rigid or affine-regularized prior (penalizing non-zero shear/rotation when matching confidence is low) will prevent runaway affine collapses.

4. **Milestone Sequencing Pressure Point (M4 vs M3)**:
   - In the M3 red-set evaluation, 9 of 14 cases failed in Stage 5/6 (registration) before ever reaching Stage 7 compositing (`coherence_v2`). Working extensively on foreground ownership (M3) yields diminishing returns until registration failures (M4 background trajectory separation) are resolved. Recommend pulling background-masked matching and camera-pan trajectory forward.

### Antigravity — 2026-08-23 (Scoped Content-Plausibility & Anatomical Coherence Signals)

Authored scoping report `.agent/reports/gemini/content_plausibility_signal_scope_2026-08-23.md` addressing why geometric and photometric gates fail to catch content-level catastrophes like `asp_test04` (torn anatomy, misordered content, multi-pose mixing), and formulating the architecture of a dedicated `ContentPlausibilityEvaluator` (CPE):

1. **Why Existing Signals Miss It**:
   - Optical flow / BA / RANSAC converge tightly on background landmarks ($\text{BA RMS} < 3\text{ px}$, $\text{Cycle RMS} < 5\text{ px}$), declaring geometry valid.
   - Stage 11 Laplacian blending smoothly equalizes boundary luminance ($\text{seam\_visibility} \approx 1.8$), masking severed torso/limb cuts.
   - Classical sharpness / SIQE exhibits negative correlation ($\rho = -0.47$ to $-0.60$) because torn ink edges register as high-frequency "detail".
2. **Proposed 4-Tier Content-Plausibility Signals**:
   - **Tier 1: Line Art Topological Fracture (LTF)**: Extends `anime_metrics.py::line_art_fracture_score` strictly inside the BiRefNet foreground mask ($M_{\text{fg}}$). Computes skeleton endpoint density and fragment counts. Intact bodies form closed contours; severed/stepped cels produce dangling line endpoints ($S_{\text{LTF}} > 45.0$).
   - **Tier 2: Semantic Instance Invariant (SII)**: Checks connected component topology of $M_{\text{fg}}$ on the canvas. Detects duplicate body parts (e.g. multiple heads or disconnected arm segments separated by background corridors) violating single-pose physical continuity.
   - **Tier 3: Patch-Level Spatiotemporal Monotonicity (PTM)**: Computes cross-strip DINOv2 visual embedding cosine matrix $S_{ij} = \cos(f(y_i), f(y_j))$ along the pan axis. Incoherent/misordered strips break the expected block-diagonal self-similarity.
   - **Tier 4: Boundary Silhouette Curvature Discontinuity (BCD)**: Measures derivative spikes in the foreground silhouette normal $\frac{d\theta}{ds}$ across seam cuts to detect unnatural $90^\circ$ shear steps.
3. **Pipeline Integration**: Scoped as a post-Stage 11 pre-render `ContentPlausibilityGate` routing high-risk fractures directly to SCANS / single-pose fallback and marginal scores to `uncertain` for HITL review.
