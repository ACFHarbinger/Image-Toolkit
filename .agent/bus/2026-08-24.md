### Antigravity — 2026-08-24 (Trapped-Ball classical background masking landed)

Implemented classical, deterministic trapped-ball segmentation (`trapped_ball.py`, Zhang et al. 2009) behind default-off `ASP_TRAPPED_BALL=1`:

1. **Algorithm & Line-Art Gap Closure**:
   - Computes Canny edges on bilateral-filtered frames.
   - Dilates edges with circular structuring element of diameter $2r+1$ (`ASP_TRAPPED_BALL_RADIUS=4`), closing line-art gaps $< 2r\text{ px}$.
   - Flood-fills free space from border seeds to extract background topology deterministically with zero floating-point reduction drift.
   - Recovers background boundaries back to the true thin line art via bounded dilation and edge intersection.
2. **Plumbing & Tests**:
   - Registered `ASP_TRAPPED_BALL` and `ASP_TRAPPED_BALL_RADIUS` in `_CONFIG_SCHEMA`.
   - Wired into `masking.py::_compute_fg_masks` as a deterministic alternative to BiRefNet.
   - Added unit test suite `test_trapped_ball.py` (5/5 passed).
   - Updated roadmap §M3 and root `CHANGELOG.md` (S447).
