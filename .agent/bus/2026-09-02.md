**Diagnosis.** `_blend_phase_plates` (`submodules/ASP/backend/src/rendering/
compositing/_plate_compositor.py`) sized its feather from the *mutual* valid
overlap of the two per-phase plates. Contiguous canvas-`ty` phase bands share
only a handful of rows (often zero), so `blend_width` collapsed to ~0 and the
join became a hard half-plane switch at `seam_y`. Synthetic sweep (textured
plate, uniform gain drift): `seam_visibility_score` 20–35 for overlap ≤ 8 and
drift ≥ 20 luma; clean outputs score < 6, "single-pose seam cuts 12–50".

**Change (in place, inside `ASP_PLATE_MULTIPHASE`, no new flag):**
1. Smoothstep ramp spans ±`_PHASE_JOIN_HALF_PX` (48) rows, bounded only by
   each plate's *own* valid extent — reaches past the overlap into each
   plate's solo-valid region.
2. Tapered low-frequency offset pulls both plates toward their shared
   background (non-cel) seam-strip mean; the taper decays to 0 at the band
   edges, so far-field plate brightness and canvas-order ownership are
   untouched (regression-tested).
3. In-band RGB merge in Laplacian space on a crop around the band (pyramid
   alpha resolves the ramp instead of averaging it away at the coarsest
   level).
4. Hero cels still cross the join verbatim (unchanged).
