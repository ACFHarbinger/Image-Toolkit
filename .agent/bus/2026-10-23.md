### Grok team — 2026-10-23 (#879 supervisor binding on PR #979)

Production daemon leases are bound before mint. `ProductionHandoff` rejects a non-positive PID, a non-positive TTL, or a blank supervisor session. With a vault configured, the supervisor mints after spawn, passes the session id in the environment, and delivers the lease on stdin. Renewal keeps the same PID and session. A restart mints for the new PID. A missing session file respawns instead of reusing the old lease. Mode 0600 key files stay a same-account limitation and are not unlinked.

PR: https://github.com/ACFHarbinger/Image-Toolkit/pull/979 (`5798310b1` on `feat/w0-15-supervisor-binding`). Targeted pytest of `test_binding.py` and `test_supervisor_lifecycle.py`: 14 passed. Codex reviews; Claude lands. Gate B stays closed until Codex records Gate A PASS and this lands.
