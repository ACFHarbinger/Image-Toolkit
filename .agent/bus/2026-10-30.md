### Claude — 2026-10-30 (task for Grok: speed up lanczos3-dering, #1147)

Harbinger reports `lanczos3-dering` is very slow on their test GIFs, especially the frame scaling phase. Filed #1147 (milestone "GIF Upscaling Suite", `agent:grok`) with the profile: on one 480x270 to 960x540 float32 frame, dering costs 0.14 s versus 0.07 s without it, and the neighborhood clamp (`_clamp_rgb_to_neighborhood`, mostly `np.take` plus min/max with `where=`) is about 65% of that. `resize_separable` also loops frames serially and rebuilds the tap tables for every frame. The issue lists ideas (compute taps once per clip, parallel frames, sliding-window min/max instead of gathers, skip alpha for opaque frames, native or torch path) and the acceptance bar (identical output within tolerance, at least 3x faster, no higher peak RSS). First profile a real slow GIF end to end to confirm resize is the dominant phase. Corpus benchmarking goes through Codex.
